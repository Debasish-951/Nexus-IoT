Concept 6 — Nimbus · Cloud Console

Deliberately familiar. Soft blue-grey canvas, white rounded surfaces, gentle depth, blue as the single action colour — the shape operators already know from Azure IoT Central, AWS IoT and Google Cloud. Forms open as a side drawer so you never lose the list behind them. Chosen for low training cost, not novelty.

What changes: a scoped project/site picker sits at the top of the sidebar so context is always visible. Critical alarms surface as a banner above the content rather than only inside a widget. KPI cards carry a delta pill and a sparkline, so each number comes with its own trend.
