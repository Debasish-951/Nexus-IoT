Concept 7 — Workbench · Master–Detail

The differentiator here is layout rather than colour. A persistent list sits beside a detail pane, so you work through items without ever navigating away and losing your filters. Nothing opens in a modal — creating a record adds a draft to the list and edits it in place. Dense 26px rows, keyboard-first (j k to move, e to acknowledge), warm neutrals with one indigo accent.

What changes: the list of dashboards stays beside the one you are viewing, so switching between them is one click and never a round trip through a menu. KPIs are a joined fact grid rather than four floating cards. A status bar at the bottom shows connection state and shortcuts permanently.
