Atlas — Calm Modern SaaS

Design philosophy: Remove every line that isn't earning its place. Atlas gets its structure from layered surfaces rather than borders — five elevation steps instead of a grid of rules — with 10px radii, generous padding, and a keyboard-first ⌘K palette as the primary way to move. It is dark-first because that is where this product already lives, so migration friction is lowest. The trade is honest: fewer rows fit on screen than Meridian.
